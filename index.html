How to use

1. Choose a timeframe. 2. Select the indicators you want. 3. Click Scan coins. A coin appears as BUY only when every selected indicator confirms. Click a coin symbol to open its Binance chart.

Indicator rules are simplified technical filters and may lag or produce false positives. Confirm signals with your own risk plan; never treat a BUY label as a guaranteed entry. Custom TradingView Pine Script must be rebuilt or connected through a supported integration.
